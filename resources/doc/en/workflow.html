Open an image in TIF / JPG / PNG format. Examine the different treatments by exploring methods and channels. Choose the size of the pen and select the option case / non case for painting the areas corresponding to the figure and the surrounding, respectively. The figure to be highlighted should be painted in red, the surrounding in blue. Evaluate the result (button "Evaluate"), and eventually, complete the supervision. The new window is removed using "C" on the keyboard. When the result looks good, push the "Continue" button and launch the full calculation At that step, you may change the blur intensity, the percentage of variance explained by PCA, and the number of channels used for calculation. Re-process to examine the effects of these changes. When the results are satifactory, check the box(es) of the image(s) you desire, and push the "Save" button. If the supervised machine learning outputs are not acceptable for your purpose, you may return to the "Color examination" tab and capture snapshots of the treatments you estimate appropriate for further manual delineation.
